# PolyKybd v0.9.44 — DOOM on the keycaps + expanded glyph scripts

> Advances the HID protocol to **v11** — update PolyKybdHost to a matching version or the keyboard reports *"Protocol mismatch."*

### 0.9.39 — Wait… is that DOOM? 😈🔥
Yes. It really runs DOOM — the full shareware first episode, playing right across the per-keycap OLEDs. Full 3D view on the master half, the slave mirroring it in lockstep, the doomguy grimacing on the status screen, and the RGB lighting punching with every shotgun blast. How you summon it is left as an exercise for the curious. 🎮

### 0.9.38 — Overlay upload fix (protocol v11) 🔌
Repacks the plain overlay-upload header to fix a 1-byte over-read. **Requires a matching host — update both.**

### 0.9.33 — 9 more keycap scripts (protocol v10) 🔤
Adds runes, Aurebesh, Standard Galactic, Cirth, IBM VGA, C64, Amiga, APL and Braille to the glyph-script override; the index is now open-ended, so future scripts need no protocol bump. **Protocol v10 — update both.**

### 0.9.25 — More Windows shortcut hints ⊞
7 more Win-chord keycap previews (volume mixer Win+Ctrl+V, Click-to-Do Win+Q, …), with exact-modifier matching.

### 0.9.24 — Fantasy keycap scripts (protocol v9) 🧝
Render your legends in Tengwar instead of your language — host-selectable, persisted, and gracefully falling back to Latin without the fantasy font pack. **Protocol v9 — needs a matching host.**

*Plus stability & security fixes and internal maintenance across the rest of 0.9.x. 🛠️*
