# PolyKybd v1.11.6 Caps Word and Auto Shift
The protocol moves to 30. Update PolyKybdHost to v1.21.14 or later first. With an older app, Caps Word works, but the Auto Shift settings are not available.

## 1.11.6: Caps Word and Auto Shift ⇧
- **Caps Word**: press both Shift keys to type the next word in capitals. The keycaps show capitals while it is on. Umlauts and the other letters of your layout are capitalised too.
- **Auto Shift**: hold a key a little longer to type its shifted character. It is off by default, and the hold time is 100 to 500 ms (default 180 ms). Turn it on under **Tap-Hold Timing…** or with `polyctl tap-terms --auto-shift on`.
