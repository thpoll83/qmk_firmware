# PolyKybd v1.13.6 Finished agents ring their key
The protocol stays at 34, so any PolyKybdHost from 1.22.16 on works with this firmware. Host 1.23.2 or later draws the Space icons for the new legend position.

## 1.13.6: A finished agent rings its key 🔔
- **Ring on finish:** when an agent finishes its turn, its number key rings three times, 4 s apart (split72). A waiting agent rings first.
- **Hold to raise:** while the key rings, hold it to bring the agent's window to the front. A tap still types the digit.

## 1.13.5: Thumb-row legends beside overlay icons
On the bottom row, a key that shows an app overlay icon draws its legend at the top left instead of in the centre, so the icon no longer cuts into ␣, ↵ or the arrows.

Plus maintenance: the HIL rig notes (#438) changed no firmware.
