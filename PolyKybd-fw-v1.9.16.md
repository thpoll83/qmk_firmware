# PolyKybd v1.9.16 Symbols and arrows in the emoji picker

**Protocol moves to 25.** Update PolyKybdHost to 1.17.83 first. Older apps still connect, but overlay keys keep their legend, and the new symbols and arrows show *Glyph missing!* because only the new app ships the font pack that has them.

## 1.9.15 – 1.9.16: Symbols and arrows in the emoji picker 🔣
- **New tab Symbols & Math (±):** superscripts and subscripts, the most used symbols, currency signs, every fraction, Greek letters, math operators and the arrows used in mathematics. A superscript or subscript key shows a full-size x in front (x², x₂), so the two are easy to tell apart.
- **Marks & Icons (✔)** keeps its marks and dingbat arrows, and adds the keyboard arrows (enter, tab, shift, page up and down, undo) and arrows in every direction in many styles.
- **Gestures & Body and Love & Celebrations are now one tab**, which makes room for the new one.
- **Page keys show where you are**, for example ▶ 3/9, in the emoji, language and Intl accent pickers.
- **A key whose glyph is missing shows *Glyph missing!*** instead of "!". This happens when the font pack that has the glyph is not on the keyboard.
- Tall symbols such as √ ∫ ⇕ are drawn smaller, so they fit the key.
- ⚠️ A recently used emoji from the old Love & Celebrations tab can show a different symbol once, because that tab moved.

## 1.9.13: Overlay keys without the key legend 🎨
An overlay can now replace a key completely, for example a game's ESC mark. Needs PolyKybdHost 1.17.83.
