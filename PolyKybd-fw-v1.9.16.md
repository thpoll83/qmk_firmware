# PolyKybd v1.9.16 New Symbols & Math tab, arrows in every direction

Update PolyKybdHost to 1.17.84 first. It ships the font pack with the new symbols and arrows; with an older app those keys show *Glyph missing!*.

## 1.9.16: New Symbols & Math tab, arrows in every direction 🔣
- **New tab Symbols & Math (±):** the superscripts and subscripts, most used symbols, currency signs, fractions, Greek letters, math operators and the arrows used in mathematics, which 1.9.15 had at the front of the Symbols tab, plus many more arrows.
- **A superscript or subscript key shows a full-size x in front** (x², x₂), so the two are easy to tell apart.
- **Marks & Icons (✔)** has the marks and dingbat arrows again, plus the keyboard arrows (enter, tab, shift, page up and down, undo) and arrows in every direction in many styles.
- **Gestures & Body and Love & Celebrations are now one tab**, which makes room for the new one.
- **Fixed: 1.9.15 cut the top or bottom off some emoji and symbols.** Tall symbols such as √ ∫ ⇕ are now also drawn smaller, so they fit the key.
- ⚠️ A recently used emoji or symbol from the old Love & Celebrations or Symbols tab can show a different one once, because those tabs changed.
