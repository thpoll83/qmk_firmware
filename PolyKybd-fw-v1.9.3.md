# PolyKybd v1.9.3 Light sensor keeps its brightness
**Protocol stays at 23.** PolyKybdHost 1.17.0 or later works with this firmware, and the two can be updated independently.

## 1.9.3: Light sensor keeps its brightness 🔆
- **With the light sensor fitted, the app's daylight brightness no longer overrides it.** Before, the app's value showed for up to half a second every ten minutes, a visible dip.
- **"Back to automatic" and the manual presets work as before.** Boards without a light sensor still follow the app's daylight brightness.
