# PolyKybd v1.9.9 Restart replies reach the app

**Protocol stays at 23.** PolyKybdHost 1.17.0 or later works with this firmware, and the two can be updated independently.

## 1.9.9: Restart replies reach the app 🔁
- **The keyboard now answers before it restarts.** This covers finishing a firmware update, changing the handedness, entering the bootloader and a reboot from the app. Before, the answer was often lost or cut off.

## 1.9.7: Faster icon fills
Overlays drawn from the icon library fill faster, and a report the other half refuses is no longer retried.

## 1.9.8: Firmware update diagnostics
When a firmware update cannot copy a block to the other half, the console now says why.
